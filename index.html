How SkillSwap Works

No money, no subscriptions. Exchange your talents in a safe student community.

1
Post Your Skills

Create your student profile. List the subjects or software you can teach, plus the skills you are eager to master this semester.

2
Find a Match

Browse the campus directory using filters for department, semester, and skill categories. Find someone whose needs match your expertise.

3
Connect & Exchange

Send a swap proposal with your proposed schedule. Meet in the campus library or jump on a video call to teach and learn!
